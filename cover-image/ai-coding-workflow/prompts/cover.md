- Connecting arrows flow left to right (data → tools → report), drawn with sketchy wavy lines and arrowheads

- Decorative: scattered hand-drawn data points, small stars, dotted grids, tiny checkmarks — the visual vocabulary of a sketchbook workflow diagram

- Sketchy, organic, slightly imperfect strokes throughout
- Variable line weight — thicker for main figures, thinner for decorative details
- Visible hand tremor in connecting lines and arrows

- Light hatching for shadow/depth on desk and main figure
- Conceptual icons with rough, hand-crafted quality
- Wavy connecting arrows between tool nodes
- No realistic depth — simple silhouettes and overlap layering
